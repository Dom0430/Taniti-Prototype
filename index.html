Frequently Requested Island Information

Important practical guidelines regarding safety, currency, power, and regulations for visitors.

Financial
Currency & Banking
Taniti uses the U.S. Dollar ($). Major credit cards are widely accepted, and local banks facilitate currency exchanges for Euros and Japanese Yen.
Electronics
Power Outlets
Standard electrical outlets are 120 volts (identical to the United States). No international plug adapters are required for US devices.
Regulations
Alcohol Ordinances & Hours
Alcohol is not served or sold between midnight and 9:00 a.m. The legal drinking age is 18.
Health & Language
Medical & English Fluency
One main hospital and several medical clinics are available. The hospital features multilingual personnel. Younger residents speak fluent English.
